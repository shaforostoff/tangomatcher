# Tango Time Travel discographies

Discographies by Tango Time Travel / Moving Art Studio ASBL, from
https://tangotimetravel.be/category/release-notes/#discographies, licensed
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: the original ODS spreadsheets (versions of 29 September 2026) were
converted to the XML format of `../xml-discographies-publicdomain`:

- dates are written as YYYY-MM-DD;
- singer and genre spellings are normalised;
- credits are split into composer and author where the source labels them,
  and otherwise kept together in `credits`;
- abbreviated composer and lyricist names ("E Donato", "C E Flores") are
  written in full where `../xml-discographies-publicdomain`,
  `../xml-lyrics-publicdomain` or the other TTT sheets leave no doubt who is
  meant: the same song credited there to one person who fits the initials
  and surname, or only one such person credited at least twice in all of
  them; and about 45 more looked up on the web for the song in question
  (mostly milongandoblog.wordpress.com's Edgardo Donato discography,
  todotango.com, el-recodo.com and Wikipedia). A surname is spelled
  differently from TTT ("L Rubinstein" -> "Luis Rubistein") only where the
  sources agree on it. Names that are still ambiguous or unknown are left as
  TTT has them;
- rows without recording data (sheet music, reissue notes) are left out;
- the few recordings by other orchestras in the Orquesta Típica Victor sheet
  have files of their own.

Each file's header comment gives its source URL, version, and the licence
and credit notes of the original. These files are under CC BY-SA 4.0, not
the licence of the rest of this repository.
